## 1. The Ship With Square Windows  — *stress concentration*
**Cast:** shipbuilder `engineer`, captain `sailor`, passengers `tourist`, `banker` · **Sets:** shipyard, ocean, ship deck, cabin
```
This ship had the biggest windows on the sea. That's exactly why it cracked.
In Puddlebrook, a shipbuilder makes a luxury ship with huge square windows. Passengers love the view.
Day one: a tiny crack… in the corner of a window. Day two: every corner. Day three: the captain is holding a window together with his hat.
So the builder tries something weird. He makes the windows… round.
No corners. No cracks. Not one.
Here's why. When a ship bends in the waves, the force flows through the metal like water in a river. A sharp corner is like a rock in the river: all the force piles up right there, and it breaks.
A circle has no corner. The force just flows around it.
That's stress concentration. It's why ships and airplanes still have round windows.
And why the captain finally got his hat back.
```

## 3. The Ketchup That Wouldn't Move — *shear-thinning fluids*
**Cast:** `chef`, `waiter`, customer `coach`, `kid` · **Sets:** busy diner, kitchen, giant bottle close-ups
```
This ketchup was a liquid. It refused to act like one.
At the Fizzwick Diner, the chef makes the best fries in town. One problem: the ketchup won't come out.

That's a shear-thinning fluid. It's why tapping the neck of the bottle works better than shaking it.
And why the chef now wears a raincoat.
```

## 6. The Bell That Changed Its Song — *Doppler effect*
**Cast:** ice-cream seller `shopkeeper`, `mayor`, kids `kid`, `kid2`, `musician` · **Sets:** village street, ice-cream cart, town square
```
The bell was perfectly tuned. Every child heard it go out of tune.

The mayor buys a new bell. Same curse. A golden bell. Same curse. The ice-cream man stops moving. The curse vanishes.
Here's why. Sound travels in waves. When the bell moves toward you, it squishes the waves together, so the note sounds higher. Moving away, it stretches them, so it sounds lower.
That's the Doppler effect. Police sirens and race cars do it too.
The cart now stays still. The kids do the running.
```

## 20. The Lens That Lit the Sea — *Fresnel lens*
**Cast:** lighthouse keeper `grandpa`, glassmaker `scientist`, captains `sailor`, `pirate` · **Sets:** stormy sea, lighthouse, glass workshop
```
The lighthouse lens was so thick, it was too heavy to carry. So they cut it into rings.
In Stormhaven, ships keep crashing on the rocks at night. The lighthouse light is weak.
The keeper orders a bigger glass lens. Too heavy for the stairs. A bigger one. Too heavy for the tower. The tower leans.
A glassmaker slices the giant lens into thin rings, keeps only the curved edges, and stacks them flat.
The light shoots twenty miles across the sea.
Here's why. Only the curved surface of a lens bends the light. The thick glass in the middle does nothing but weigh a ton. Rings keep the curves, and drop the weight.
That's a Fresnel lens. It saved thousands of ships.
The keeper still uses the old lens. As a fish bowl.
```
