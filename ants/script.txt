Nobody was in charge of the ants. They still found the shortest road.
In Honkford, the mayor builds a perfect road system. Forty traffic lights. Zero jams. That was the plan.
Day one: a jam on Main Street. Day two: a jam everywhere. Day three: the mayor is stuck in his own parade.
Then one ant finds his sandwich. She walks home… leaving a scent.
Short road? Ants return fast, scent gets strong. Long road? Ants return late, scent fades.
Soon every ant takes the shortest road. No boss. No lights.
So the engineer does something weird. He follows the ants… and paints the road where they walk.
Here's why. Each ant smells only the ground under her. But the best road smells strongest, so everyone picks it. Tiny choices make a smart crowd.
That's swarm intelligence. Delivery trucks still use it.
And the mayor? He followed the ants home.
